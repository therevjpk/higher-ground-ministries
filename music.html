The album · John P. Kennon

We Will Overcome

We Will Overcome is a gospel album with a Southern road under it: songs of victory through the blood of the Lamb, written for anyone fighting their way from trouble to testimony.

About the album

We Will Overcome is a collection of songs born out of thirty years of living, losing, and holding on. Rooted in Scripture and carried by gospel, country, and Southern rock with strong vocal harmony, it moves from bold declarations of victory through the blood of the Lamb (We Will Overcome, More Than Conquerors, I Am a Soldier) to quiet prayers for God to break up the hard ground of the heart (Fallow Ground, Closer to Your Throne).

It walks the prodigal road home in Back Home and Porch Light On, sits with grief and the hope of heaven in Angels Wings and I Hope You’re There, and celebrates the simple gift of love and life in Sunrise and Sounds Like Livin’. Through it all runs one message: whatever you’ve been through, you can still rise. Through Jesus, we will overcome.

Each song has a one-minute preview.
